# VitalKin — Play Store listing

## Short description (≤ 80 chars)

Track your family's blood pressure, sugar & weight — privately, on-device.

## Long description

VitalKin is a simple family health log. Add the people you care for — parents,
grandparents, kids, yourself — and keep their health readings in one place.

Log blood pressure, blood sugar (fasting or post-meal) and weight in seconds.
Each reading is dated, and VitalKin flags values outside the healthy range so
you can spot problems early.

See the trend with clean charts for every metric, set daily medicine
reminders so nobody misses a dose, and generate a PDF report to hand to the
doctor — or share readings as a CSV with family.

Everything is stored only on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no
sample data. Your family's health stays yours.

VitalKin is a personal health log, not a medical device or diagnosis tool.
Always check important values with a clinician.

## Feature bullets

- Log blood pressure, blood sugar and weight per family member
- Healthy-range flags (Normal / Elevated / Borderline / High) on every reading
- Trend charts with healthy-range reference lines
- Daily medicine reminders with local notifications
- Doctor-ready PDF report, shareable from the app
- CSV export to share readings with family
- 100% on-device storage — no account, no cloud, no ads
